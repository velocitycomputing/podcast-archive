## Summary

Mary Harris and Romeo Krokiatsky, managing editor of New Voice of Ukraine and co-host of Ukraine Without Hype, examined whether the United States has abandoned Ukraine, citing The Atlantic’s claim that America is now an ally of Russia, a New York Times opinion line that Russia was losing until Trump entered, and Treasury Secretary Scott Besant’s evasive Meet the Press answers on whether Ukraine is an ally. They discussed Trump’s October 9 deal to buy Russian diesel—about 300,000 tons, or 2.25 million barrels, less than half a day of U.S. demand—as a cash infusion to Russia, with claims that Trump-linked holding companies, frozen Lukoil assets, and Qatari and Saudi investors could benefit; Trump’s Truth Social framing of the purchase as rewarding Russia for not helping Iran; diesel prices near six dollars a gallon, almost double a year earlier, amid midterm politics; and an executive order suspending the Graham Act sanctions two days before they were to take effect, despite Rep. Don Beyer’s warning that the bill let Trump ignore it. The conversation also covered Ukraine’s temporary loss of Starshield access, Wall Street Journal reporting that Elon Musk slowed Starlink after a Trump call, the firing of Defense Minister Mihaila Fedorov and Kyiv protests, Palantir and Alex Kemp ties, reported U.S. restraint on Patriot missiles, Putin’s winter bombing of fragile energy infrastructure, worsening winters since 2022–23, widespread EcoFlow backup batteries, fading Ukrainian humor, Romeo’s move from Kyiv after nearby strikes, his son Imriel, Krokiatsky’s argument that Trump has a Russian “might makes right” outlook and that Ukrainians see his actions as betrayal, and Zelensky’s meeting with envoy Steve Wickoff.
