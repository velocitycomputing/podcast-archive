## Summary

Tim Ferriss and Kevin discuss a recent inflection in AI personal assistants, naming Instinct, Meta’s Muse, Grokbot, and ChatGPT’s Dots, and describe Instinct as an iMessage-style agent that can be authorized to Gmail/Google Drive/calendar, proactively flags documents and prescriptions, changes flights, books hard OpenTable reservations, and coordinates with friends’ agents; they claim Instinct reached a $10 billion valuation in four months, Muse has been the number-one app-store app for weeks, and Grokbot is less mature. They extend the discussion to agentic commerce and headless software, citing Amazon blocking Meta’s agent because sponsored placements and ads could be bypassed, Shopify welcoming agents, Blackmagic’s DaVinci Resolve MCP server enabling ChatGPT to edit a podcast, and similar control of Photoshop/CAD, with Henry Schookman, Apple Intelligence, Gemini, ChatGPT, and Anthropic framed as future entry points. The transcript also covers Tim’s new book preface and early Dexcom continuous glucose monitor memories, then turns to supplements: urolithin A for mitophagy, with a claim that only 12 of 100 adults had detectable levels and Tim takes 1,000 mg/day of Timeline’s MitoPure; beta-alanine for climbing, citing a randomized double-blind study of 15 elite climbers taking 4 g/day for four weeks, with 57% longer time before falling versus 19% for placebo, 21 additional seconds beyond placebo, and 10 versus 4 added moves on longer traverses, while Tim takes about 6.4 g/day in sustained-release form to reduce paresthesia; plus Mementis collagen peptides plus vitamin C before training, psyllium husk heavy-metal concerns with Yerba Prima/Organic India as better-tested options, seaweed chips and microplastics, and creatine’s GI/weight-retention tradeoffs.

For the user, the practical move is to treat AI assistants as both productivity and privacy decisions: if already paying for ChatGPT, try its agent features; if wanting an easier public option, test Meta’s Muse; if seeking the most talked-about agent, seek an Instinct invite; and wait on Grokbot unless its model improves. Before connecting Gmail, Drive, calendar, OpenTable, or payment accounts, decide the acceptable blast radius, use separate offline storage for sensitive files, ask what data is retained, whether memories/history can be exported, and whether the provider has a documented security model; also consider that agentic shopping may shift deals away from Amazon-style sponsored listings, so compare AI-recommended retailers like Walmart/Shopify merchants rather than assuming one destination site is cheapest. Health-wise, urolithin A is a research-backed but sponsor-adjacent supplement to evaluate at 1,000 mg/day if interested in mitochondrial/mitophagy support; beta-alanine is a concrete climbing/endurance lead, with 4 g/day in the study and sustained-release dosing to reduce tingling, but it requires 2–3 weeks of loading and may not help very short bouldering; collagen peptides plus vitamin C before training is another lead, with hide collagen preferred over bone to reduce contaminant risk. If using psyllium husk, choose brands with heavy-metal testing and add water; avoid seaweed chips if microplastics are a concern; avoid creatine plus espresso/MCT oil before airport travel; and do not casually use prescription sedatives like gabapentin for flights without clinician guidance.
