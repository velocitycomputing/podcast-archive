## Summary

Argentina's inflation fell from 25.5% (December 2023) to 1.7% (December 2024) through government budget balancing—a win for austerity as policy, though the US cultural reaction would likely resemble French-style protests. The real barrier to middle-class recovery isn't wealthy people getting rich, but job outsourcing; manufacturing must return domestically (Hamiltonian economics) to create sustainable wages, not just rely on wealth "trickling down." AI agents (Meta's Muse, soon able to move money automatically) will trigger banking disruption within a decade: they'll sweep household cash into high-yield accounts, forcing a bank-run scenario that breaks the current cartel where banks don't compete. Meanwhile, long-term unemployment (6+ months or longer) surged 82% from February 2023 to August 2024, reaching 1.93 million Americans, while the official 4.1% jobless rate hides the real picture—actual unemployment is closer to 12% when counting discouraged workers who stopped looking—and job listings themselves are often fake, pre-filled, or rotated with no real hiring intent.

**Actionable: Invest heavily in stocks and assets now to protect against inflation from deficit spending—holding cash guarantees you'll be "stolen from by the government." Expect banking sector upheaval as AI automation hits: ensure your deposits are positioned for the coming competition and higher yields. For employment, treat the job market as genuinely broken despite headline statistics; expect extended searches and don't rely on gig work as primary income. If building a business, recognize that productive wealth creation and domestic job-building are what drive real economic gains, making policy support for manufacturing and deregulation essential.**
