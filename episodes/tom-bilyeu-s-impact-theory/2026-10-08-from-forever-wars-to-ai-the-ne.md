## Summary

Dixon argues that the global elite have transitioned from extracting wealth through forever wars to using AI as their primary tool, enabled by aligned incentives rather than explicit coordination. Rising energy costs force the Fed to raise interest rates, which pushes ordinary people into unaffordable debt on mortgages and refinances, forcing them to sell assets that are immediately purchased by the connected wealthy—who can borrow against assets and access them before retirement. Meanwhile, AI creates massive productivity gains that benefit asset owners and businesses while automating jobs (taxi drivers, truckers) for those without ownership. The Federal Reserve, owned by member banks that must buy government debt and create money, operates as a quasi-private mechanism that perfectly hedges the FIC (Financial-Industrial Complex) across all scenarios: whether markets rise, fall, or crash, asset managers like BlackRock profit through fees, equity appreciation, or private equity acquisitions. This system drives 88% of stock market ownership into 10% of households.

Your actionable choice is binary: either own AI-leveraged assets and a business (using AI to accelerate productivity and borrow against external assets), or face automation and dependence on universal basic income with no asset ownership and full surveillance compliance. Immediately register your side hustle as a legitimate business structure to access business banking and borrowing capability. If you have spare capital, invest it outside tax-deferred accounts so you can leverage it against assets before retirement—pension contributions alone keep you waiting until 65 while wages lag inflation. Most critically, understand the wealth transfer mechanics (debt→forced selling→concentrated ownership) so you can position assets and income streams before the job losses accelerate over the next five years.
