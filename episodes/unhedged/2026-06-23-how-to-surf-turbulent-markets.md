## Summary

The AI boom's volatility is causing significant equity market swings, with investor Ruchir Sharma (chair of Rockefeller International and author of *What Went Wrong with Capitalism*) advising on navigating this turbulence during a discussion recorded at the FT Weekend Festival in New York. Sharma's perspective was featured alongside hosts Katie Martin and Rob Armstrong, who specifically recommended "going long quality stocks that are out of the tech spotlight" and "shorting sharks" (interpreted as shorting speculative or overvalued assets within the AI sector). The episode frames the current market environment as a period where traditional tech dominance is being disrupted, requiring a shift in investment focus.

Investors should actively consider reallocating capital toward high-quality, non-technology stocks currently undervalued due to the AI frenzy, such as those in consumer staples, healthcare, or industrials with strong fundamentals. Simultaneously, they should identify and short speculative assets within the AI space—like unprofitable startups with inflated valuations—that lack sustainable business models, using Sharma's framework to distinguish between genuine innovation and market hype. Follow-up research should focus on identifying specific quality stocks with resilient earnings and low debt, while evaluating AI-related companies for concrete revenue streams versus pure narrative-driven growth.
