## Summary

Dr. Kyle Gillett, a dual board-certified physician specializing in hormone optimization, discusses evidence-based approaches to optimizing male hormones throughout life. The episode covers foundational assessment through blood tests, dietary strategies including Vitamin D and fiber intake, and the impact of caloric restriction, stress management, and exercise on hormone levels. Gillett addresses testosterone replacement therapy considerations for young adults, examines supplement efficacy including creatine, L-carnitine, betaine, Tongkat Ali, and Fadogia Agrestis with specific dosing guidance, and addresses clinical concerns including alcohol's suppressive effect on testosterone via aromatase, prostate health management using tadalafil for urination symptoms, and hair loss interventions with finasteride or dutasteride.

Actionable steps include obtaining baseline bloodwork to measure current hormone status, optimizing dietary intake of Vitamin D and fiber while managing caloric intake appropriately, incorporating resistance exercise, and evaluating supplementation with creatine and L-carnitine (noting TMAO/garlic interactions). For those experiencing hair loss or prostate concerns, consider finasteride/dutasteride and tadalafil respectively. If considering testosterone optimization, consult with a qualified physician about whether TRT or alternatives like clomiphene (a SERM) suit your situation. Additionally, prioritize stress reduction and establishing life purpose as foundational hormone-optimization strategies, and moderate alcohol consumption to prevent testosterone suppression.
