## Summary

The episode covered the transition of enterprise AI from experimental pilots to cost-effective production scale. OpenAI and Microsoft both showcased their next-generation approaches—OpenAI expanding Codex beyond developers while Microsoft prioritized lower-cost, customizable frontier models. KPMG research revealed that the highest-impact AI users treat AI as a reasoning partner rather than a tool, and these skills can be taught at scale across organizations. Additional industry news included Trump's AI executive order, Anthropic's expanded Mythos access, and SK Hynix's plans to double memory chip capacity.

To act on this, assess whether your organization is still in experimentation mode or ready to scale. If ready, focus on treating AI as a reasoning partner for your teams—invest in upskilling employees to work effectively with AI systems rather than simply implementing AI tools. Evaluate whether lower-cost, customizable models (like Microsoft's offerings) better fit your budget than premium solutions, and consider which specific enterprise AI tools address your workflow gaps: workforce transformation, low-code agent building, customer experience optimization, or knowledge work automation.
