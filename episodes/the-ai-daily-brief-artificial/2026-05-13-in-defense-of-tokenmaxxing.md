## Summary

NLW challenges the common criticism of tokenmaxxing by arguing it reflects a necessary shift in enterprise AI strategy. As companies transition from assisted AI to agentic AI, aggressive token expenditure isn't wasteful—it's a learning cost that separates winners from laggards. While token leaderboards can incentivize poor spending habits, organizations that experiment liberally and learn from mistakes will outpace competitors optimizing prematurely for ROI.
