## Summary

May 2026 marked a pivotal shift in AI economics as the industry transitioned from subsidized pricing to a token-scarcity model characterized by usage-based billing and enterprise cost pressures. The episode argues that future AI competition will be determined by organizations' ability to access, afford, optimize, and deploy AI tokens effectively, moving away from the previous era of cheap compute abundance toward a resource-constrained reality where token efficiency becomes a competitive differentiator.

For enterprise decision-makers, the immediate priority is developing internal capabilities around AI token optimization and cost management. Research from KPMG shows that the highest-impact AI users treat the technology as a reasoning partner—a skill that can be taught at scale—making workforce training in responsible AI deployment critical for managing both costs and ROI in this new environment.
