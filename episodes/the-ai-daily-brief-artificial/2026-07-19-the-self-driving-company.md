## Summary

Replit's internal AI agents have nearly tripled engineering output without quality loss, but the broader takeaway is how AI is reshaping entire organizational structures. The episode explores building "self-driving organizations" by connecting agents across business systems and creating continuous loops that automatically turn goals and customer feedback into action.

For your organization, the actionable approach is to treat AI agents as reasoning partners (a skill KPMG research shows can be taught at scale) and architect them to work across your business systems—not as isolated tools. Focus on establishing feedback loops that connect customer input and strategic goals directly to automated action, moving beyond one-off productivity gains toward systemic organizational redesign.
