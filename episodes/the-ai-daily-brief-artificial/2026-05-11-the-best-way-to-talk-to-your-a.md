## Summary

The episode examines the emerging debate over Markdown versus HTML as the optimal format for agent handoffs, arguing that this technical choice reflects a deeper philosophical shift in how organizations approach AI—moving from producing finished outputs to structuring inputs that enable agents to generate their own outputs. This reframing introduces "agent management" as a new skill set. The episode also covers major industry news including Anthropic's anticipated pre-IPO funding round, surging demand for Cerebras IPO shares, TSMC hitting capacity limits, Apple's preliminary chipmaking agreement with Intel, and OpenAI's launch of a new Chrome plugin for Codex.

For practical application, if you're deploying AI agents in your workflow, you should experiment with how you format instructions and data handoffs—whether Markdown's simplicity or HTML's structure better helps your agents parse and act on the information. More broadly, assess your organization's agentic AI strategy using frameworks like KPMG's "Agentic AI Untangled" to decide whether you should build custom agents, purchase existing solutions, or partner with providers. Additionally, if you're in software development or compliance work, monitoring the emerging tools and platforms (like those featured in the sponsors) can help you identify where agentic solutions might accelerate your team's productivity, particularly as the market for agent management tools stabilizes.
