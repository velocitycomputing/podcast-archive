## Summary

NLW and Nufar Gaspar discuss why executives adopting AI sets the strongest signal for organizational adoption, then identify four "digital employees" leaders should deploy immediately: a research analyst to gather and synthesize information, a strategic thought partner to challenge thinking and explore scenarios, a communication expert to draft and refine messaging, and an operational powerhouse to handle scheduling, task management, and workflow optimization. The episode frames AI not as a future capability but as immediately deployable systems that extend executive capacity.

To act: Start by defining one gap in your own workflow—research synthesis, strategic brainstorming, writing/editing, or task management—and build a prompt-based "digital employee" for that role using an LLM. Test it for two weeks on real work before expanding. Document what works and use that as a template for your leadership team to adopt similar roles, creating a repeatable model that demonstrates value before asking broader teams to invest in AI systems.
