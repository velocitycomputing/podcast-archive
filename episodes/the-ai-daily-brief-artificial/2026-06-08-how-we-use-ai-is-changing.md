## Summary

The episode highlights a fundamental shift in how AI is being used: away from standalone chatbots toward agents, coding tools, and automated loops. ChatGPT's rumored "super app" redesign exemplifies this trend, which creates a widening performance gap between casual users (experiencing linear gains from occasional chat use) and power users leveraging agents (seeing compounding gains). Key industry news includes Trump exploring government investment in AI labs, Google renting SpaceX compute resources, and NVIDIA securing memory supply from SK Hynix. Research from KPMG and the University of Texas at Austin reveals that the highest-impact AI users treat AI as a reasoning partner rather than just a tool, suggesting these advanced usage patterns can be taught and scaled.

To capture compounding gains from AI, shift from passive chat interactions to agent-based workflows that automate reasoning loops and iterative tasks. Develop skills in treating AI as a reasoning partner—framing problems collaboratively rather than asking for answers. Evaluate whether platforms like Outsystems, Zenflow Work, or other agent-building tools fit your workflow to create self-reinforcing AI-assisted processes, rather than relying on one-off queries that plateau in value over time.
