## Summary

Four new AI models—GPT Live, Grok 4.5, Cognition SWE-1.7, and GPT-5.6 Sol—each represent a distinct approach to AI-driven work. GPT Live introduces natural voice assistants, Cognition SWE-1.7 offers ultra-fast coding agents, while the other models deliver cheaper implementation options and powerful general-purpose workhorses. The episode analyzes how each model signals different priorities and future trajectories for AI tool design.

To apply this immediately: evaluate which of these four models addresses your most pressing workflow bottleneck—real-time conversation (GPT Live), code generation speed (SWE-1.7), budget constraints, or general reasoning tasks. Rather than choosing one model, plan how to combine them across different work categories: use the specialized tools where they excel (voice for hands-free tasks, coding agents for development sprints) and reserve general models for reasoning work that benefits from broader context. This layered approach mirrors KPMG's research finding that the highest-impact AI users treat AI as a reasoning partner across complementary tools, not as a single replacement.
