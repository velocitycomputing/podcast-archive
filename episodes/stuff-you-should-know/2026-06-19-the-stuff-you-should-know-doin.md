## Summary

The episode discusses Occam's Razor, the logical principle stating that the simplest explanation is usually the correct one. Originally conceived as a guide for evaluating arguments, the razor has been increasingly misused over time—transformed from a precision tool for logical reasoning into a "broadsword" wielded to summarily disprove opposing viewpoints without genuine engagement or evidence review.

The actionable takeaway is learning to identify when someone is invoking Occam's Razor as a fake skeptic: watch for people using it to dismiss ideas prematurely without actually examining the evidence or engaging with competing explanations on their merits. True application of the principle should involve careful reasoning, not reflexive dismissal.
