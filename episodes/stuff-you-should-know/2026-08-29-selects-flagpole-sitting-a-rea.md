## Summary

Stuff You Should Know hosts Josh Clark and Chuck Bryant, with Jerry, frame a July 14, 2020 episode around Harvey Danger’s “Flagpole Sitter” and the 1920s American fad of flagpole sitting. They identify Alvin “Shipwreck” Kelly—born Alawishas Anthony Kelly in 1893 in Hell’s Kitchen, with an orphaned childhood and self-reported work as a steeplejack, stunt person, pilot, and boxer—as the likely originator after a January 1924 publicity stunt lasting 13 hours and 13 minutes, possibly in Hollywood or Philadelphia.
