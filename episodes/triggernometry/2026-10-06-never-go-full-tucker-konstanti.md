## Summary

Konstantin Kisin's Triggernometry monologue, "Never Go Full Tucker," reviews the Tucker Carlson and Jacob Rees-Mogg exchange on World War II. Kisin says it was less outrageous than the clips suggested. He calls it disappointing, and describes Carlson as ideologically captured and not very bright, not a secret Nazi. He extends his "woke right" idea through horseshoe theory. Both the woke left and the woke right judge historical actors against a perfect world, conclude they were monsters, and ignore the choices those actors actually faced. Kisin rebuts several of Carlson's points:
- **"Killing innocents is wrong":** He calls this true but childish as a framing of WWII. The real question was whether to use force to stop Axis mass killing that was already under way, knowing the force would kill some civilians too. His examples are 6 million Jews, millions of Slavs, and millions of Chinese civilians killed before 1939.
- **The Mosley internment:** Oswald Mosley was interned in May 1940, with invasion expected within weeks. He had married Diana Mitford at Goebbels' house with Hitler as a guest, and Diana was lobbying for a pro-Nazi radio station. Kisin notes Mosley was released in 1943 and lived until 1980.
- **"Hitler never wanted war with Britain":** Kisin says that is an implausible reason to trust Hitler. He argues Hitler wanted European hegemony, which Britain had opposed for 400 years, and that Hitler turned on the Soviet Union despite their pact. He also points out that Carlson defends Russia's concern about its borders in Ukraine but not Britain's in 1939.
- **"We'd all be speaking German":** Kisin reads this as shorthand for living under exterminationist tyranny. He says "why is your country so screwed?" moves the goalposts, because the real alternative was a Hitlerian empire, not peace.
