## Summary

Konstantin Kisin uses this Triggernometry episode to answer a Sunday Times piece by former PM Rishi Sunak, which quoted him without naming him over a February 2025 on-air remark that Sunak couldn't be English because he is "a brown Hindu." Kisin concedes the phrasing was a poor shortcut for "British Asian." He says he texted Sunak privately to apologise and got no reply. He rejects the charge that his position is racist. His argument is that English is an ethnic identity and British a civic one. He notes his own children are British but not English, and says Suella Braverman (whom he calls Ella) made a similar point about herself. He treats Sunak's view, that anyone born in England who feels English is English, as the real issue. In his telling it leaves ethnic English people as the only group denied an ethnic identity, which feeds a "two-tier" grievance and the ethno-nationalist right. He cites Katharine Birbalsingh's upcoming book *Diversity Is Not Our Strength*, with its examples of ethnic self-segregation in schools. He also cites a girl sent home from a school "culture day" for speaking about Britain in a Union Jack dress. He blames identity politics, rapid demographic change and mass immigration for the current debate. His proposed fix follows the imperial model of an ethnic core plus a civic identity (Russky vs. Rossiyanin, with Khabib as the example). English, Scottish, Welsh and Northern Irish identities would be recognised as the founding ethnic groups, and a shared "Britishness" would be promoted.
