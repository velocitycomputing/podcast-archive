A.G. Sulzberger's appeal to support original journalism introduces a discussion of Democrats' historic midterm victories in Kansas, Texas, Iowa, South Carolina, and Ohio governorships, plus a potentially competitive Mississippi Senate race, which Ezra Klein and Aaron Redica interpret as proof that the red-blue map is fake and a Big Tent is real. They claim Trump led Republicans to defeat by raising costs through tariffs, pursuing new wars, creating border disorder with ICE, and refusing to pivot, while Democrats won by listening, staying focused on affordability and the Iran War, and fielding candidates who fit their states: Josh Turek, Adam Hamilton, James Tallarico, Dr. Annie Andrews, Scott Collum, Roy Cooper (+20), John Ossif (+18), Bobby Polito (+25), and others.
