## Summary

This episode is a short, monologue-style segment from Steve Schmidt's "The Warning," released on the eve of the 25th anniversary of September 11th. Schmidt frames 9/11 as the event that set in motion the chain leading to Trump. He opens with a historical analogy: in 1934 the Nazis rewrote the words of "Silent Night" to invoke Hitler's protection, which he compares to what he calls Trump's desecrations, tolerated by his supporters. He argues that cults of personality (Mussolini, Hitler, Jim Jones) are always deadly. The bulk of the material is a critique of a Republican rally or convention in Dallas, which he calls a "travesty" and "freak show." He cites Trump's promise of a "$5,000 Trump dividend" funded by tariffs and Lindsey Graham's speech casting Democrats as haters of American culture. He says nobody can tell Trump "no" anymore. He also accuses the cabinet of corruption, says Trump has declared victory 66 times in a war the country is losing, and says gas prices did not fall as promised. Schmidt cites the interrogator of Adolf Eichmann, who said democracy keeps "little Eichmanns" harmless while dictatorship makes them deadly. Schmidt applies this to Trump's circle. He predicts "the end is nigh for MAGA," with subpoenas, investigations and an "era of accountability" to follow, and closes by promoting his Substack membership.
