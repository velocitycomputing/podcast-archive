## Summary

Kanika Batra, diagnosed with antisocial personality disorder, discussed the lived reality of sociopathy with host Chris Will. Topics included what emotions sociopaths actually experience (selective rather than absent), whether antisocial traits are inborn or developed, her attraction to revenge and status, dating dynamics from a sociopath's perspective, power dynamics with men, her conversion to Christianity, and practical markers for identifying sociopathic individuals. The episode also covered her marriage, manipulation tactics in dating, breakups, and how sociopaths understand themselves.

For listeners dating or considering dating: the episode's core actionable insight is recognizing red flags of sociopathic behavior (detailed at 01:33:48) rather than hoping self-awareness or relationship commitment will change someone's underlying wiring. Women should distinguish between genuinely healthy dating advice and manipulative tactics that exploit male attraction to dominance (01:27:35 onwards). If you're already with someone displaying sociopathic traits, understand they experience relationships fundamentally differently—as transactional rather than reciprocal—making the standard advice to "communicate more" ineffective as a fix.
