## Summary

Sam Harris and former US Attorney Preet Bharara examine the deterioration of good-faith political discourse in America, focusing on media fragmentation, controversial prosecutions of political opponents, and how high-profile cases like Trump's indictments paradoxically strengthened rather than weakened his political position. They explore the distinction between lying and bullshit, analyze tribalism and dogmatism across the political spectrum, critique Democratic Party failures and hypocrisy, and discuss how institutional norms have eroded—examining specific examples including the politicized treatment of Anthony Fauci and concerns about Trump's self-dealing.

The episode's relevance lies in understanding how polarization mechanisms operate: recognizing how media silos enable tribal thinking, distinguishing between deliberate falsehood and reckless dishonesty, and identifying why prosecutorial approaches may backfire politically. For someone concerned with institutional integrity and the 2028 election, Bharara's perspective as a seasoned prosecutor offers insight into rule-of-law failures, and the conversation models how to hold competing concerns simultaneously—pursuing accountability while avoiding appearance of political persecution and restoring norms without sanctimony.

## Transcript

Sam Harris speaks with Preet Bharara about the collapse of good faith debate in American politics. They discuss the fragmentation of media, the treatment of Anthony Fauci, the rush to prosecute political opponents, Trump’s self-dealing, why indictments strengthened Trump, lying vs bullshit, tribalism and dogmatism, the failures of the Democratic Party, hypocrisy and sanctimony, restoring norms and institutions, the 2028 election, and other topics.
This episode was originally recorded for the Stay Tuned with Preet podcast.
Preet Bharara is the former United States Attorney for the Southern District of New York (SDNY) and is currently a partner at the law firm WilmerHale. Before serving as US Attorney, Bharara was Chief Counsel to Senator Charles Schumer, the current US Senate Minority Leader. He wrote the New York Times best-seller Doing Justice: A Prosecutor’s Thoughts on Crime, Punishment and the Rule of Law. Bharara also shares his perspectives as host of the Stay Tuned with Preet and Insider podcasts on the Vox Media Podcast Network.
