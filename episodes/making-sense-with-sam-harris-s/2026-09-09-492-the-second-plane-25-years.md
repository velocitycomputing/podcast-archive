## Summary

In episode #492 of Making Sense (the 25th anniversary of 9/11), Sam Harris argues that the second plane showed the attacks were intentional and carried out by people willing to die, which points to sincere religious belief rather than madness, poverty or despair. He says 12 of the 19 hijackers attended college, and that their letters, videos and instructions framed the attacks as worship aimed at martyrdom and paradise. He grants that the US response was costly: roughly $8 trillion, an estimated 20,000–50,000 civilian deaths directly and about ten times that from the civil wars that followed, a bungled Afghanistan withdrawal, and the abandonment of Afghan allies. He still says these failures don't answer the question of jihadism. By his account, bin Laden is dead, jihadists have killed only a few hundred people in the US in 25 years, and RAND-type analyses credit counterterrorism with stopping about 100 attacks. He treats October 7, 2023 as continuous with 9/11 and says the Western reaction was worse than in 2001. He describes an identitarian "oppressor/oppressed" framework in elite universities and criticizes figures such as Zohran Mamdani and Abdul El-Sayed. He argues that "Islamophobia" conflates criticism of ideas with bigotry against people, and that Islam, being a proselytizing set of ideas, is open to criticism like communism. He supports ideological vetting in immigration and welcoming Muslim dissidents, reformers and apostates. He closes with a pitch for listener support, the new Making Sense community, and a promise of no ads or sponsors. The transcript is truncated before the end of that pitch.

This is an opinion monologue, so there are no steps to follow, and the claims are Harris's own, not settled fact. To act on it, check the specific factual claims you care about against primary sources: the RAND counterterrorism figures, the civilian-death estimates, the hijackers' education and background data, and the Michigan synagogue incident and the statements attributed to El-Sayed and Mamdani. Treat the sweeping claims about Islam and elite institutions as contested arguments, since some are generalizations stated more confidently than the evidence in the episode supports. If you want to engage with the show, the community forum is under the community tab at samharris.org and in the Making Sense community app. Paid access covers full episodes, bonus content and early tour tickets. Harris previously covered the same ground in a 20th-anniversary episode, also titled "The Second Plane", and in his book *The End of Faith*. Those are the places to look for the longer version of the argument.
