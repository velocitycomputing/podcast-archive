## Summary

Dave Davies interviewed Steve Buscemi, age 68, about his current roles in The Only Living Pickpocket in New York, directed by Noah Sagan and starring John Turturro as an aging pickpocket in a cashless, traceable-phone world, and Wild Horse Nine, directed by Martin McDonagh and set in 1973 Chile before the coup against Salvador Allende, where Buscemi plays CIA official MJ supervising John Malkovich and Sam Rockwell’s agents on Easter Island. The conversation covered his more than 100 film/TV credits, including Reservoir Dogs, Fargo, The Sopranos, and Boardwalk Empire, his writing/directing/starring in Trees Lounge, his four years as an FDNY firefighter at Engine 55, his father’s role steering him toward civil service and acting, a childhood bus accident at age 4 that fractured his skull and produced a $6,000 settlement he used for a car and about $1,500 of Lee Strasberg Institute classes, and his 9/11 return to Ground Zero after five Engine 55 members were killed. Buscemi also discussed working with the Coen brothers, Quentin Tarantino, Terence Winter, and Martin Scorsese, playing Mr. Pink and Carl Showalter, and being cast as Nucky Thompson in Boardwalk Empire despite initial doubts because the real Nucky Johnson was a larger man.
