## Summary

Kara Swisher explores longevity and healthspan by examining wellness trends including GLP-1s, peptides, and hyperbaric chambers, then cutting through the noise with expert guidance. The episode features Dr. David Agus (founding CEO of the Ellison Medical Institute), Dr. Ezekiel Emanuel (oncologist and author of *Eat Your Ice Cream: Six Simple Rules for a Long and Healthy Life*), Dr. David Daniels (Kara's cardiologist who repaired her 2011 heart defect), and Jason Braun (her personal trainer), who provide a roundtable discussion on evidence-based longevity strategies.

The actionable insight is that the most effective approach to living longer is deliberately unsexy: the basics you already know work. Rather than pursuing trendy medical interventions, the episode's experts emphasize fundamental health practices, with Dr. Emanuel's book title itself suggesting that longevity comes from simple, consistent rules. Listeners should expect practical guidance on decision-making from Kara's own medical team rather than endorsements of expensive or novel biohacking methods.
