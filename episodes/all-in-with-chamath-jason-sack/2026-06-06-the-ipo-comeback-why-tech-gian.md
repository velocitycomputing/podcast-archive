## Summary

The episode features Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman and Planet Labs CEO Will Marshall discussing their paths to going public and the IPO market recovery. Key discussion points include how IPO status impacts employees, customers, and daily business operations; timelines for deploying datacenters in space; Cerebras's AI-focused silicon business and how artificial intelligence is reshaping the semiconductor market; and the founder-CEO perspective on managing liquidity decisions throughout the growth journey.

Actionable takeaways for tech leaders include: understanding the employee and customer communication strategy required when going public (from the lived experience of two active founders); getting realistic timelines for moonshot infrastructure projects like orbital computing; and learning how to balance liquidity needs (founder and employee equity value realization) with maintaining operational control and long-term vision—directly applicable whether you're building toward an IPO or evaluating whether to go public at all.
